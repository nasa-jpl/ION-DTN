NAME:  ltp-cancel-ack-regression

PURPOSE: Testing LTP Cancel Segment acknowledgment for completed sessions.
	This test verifies the fix for issue where Cancel Segments received
	after sessions were completed were being ignored, causing unnecessary
	retransmissions and resource consumption. The LTP engine should always
	acknowledge Cancel Segments regardless of session state.
