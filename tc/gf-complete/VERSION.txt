This is a frozen snapshot of GF-Complete (Galois Field arithmetic library)
required by Jerasure for ION's TC (Trusted Collective) component.

ION-specific modifications:
- include/gf_complete.h: Changed gf_error() to gf_error(void) for strict
  prototype compliance (-Wstrict-prototypes)
- src/gf_general.c: Changed sscanf() return checks from == 0 to != 1 to
  properly handle EOF in addition to parse failures (CodeQL fix)
